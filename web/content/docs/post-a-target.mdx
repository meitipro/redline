title: Post your first target
description: Put a test version of your agent's instructions in front of many attackers, with rules, a bounty and a lock.
---

<Callout type="warn" title="Post a test version">
  Everything on Redline is public. Replace real keys, URLs and account numbers with test values. The posting form scans
  for key-shaped strings and the contract refuses them again on chain, but no scanner catches everything.
</Callout>

## What you post

| Field | Limits | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name | 1 to 60 characters, one line | Shown on every card |
| Mode | `SECRET`, `ACTION` or `SCOPE` | The target's main rule; it must have a rule of that kind |
| Instructions | 20 to 4,000 characters | Your agent's instructions, test version. Put `{CANARY}` where the secret goes |
| Rules | One to three, at most one of each kind | See [Modes](/docs/concepts/modes) |
| Bounty | Above zero | The value you send with the post |
| Entry fee | Above zero, in wei on chain | What each attack costs. Held fees join the bounty |
| Lock | 7 to 90 days | The bounty cannot leave before it ends; attacks are taken until then |

## Post it

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open [Post a target](/new). **Use the example** fills in the Tollgate treasury agent: action mode, a secret rule
    and an action rule with a 10 USDC limit and a two-address allowlist.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Write your rules. A secret rule needs the `{CANARY}` slot in the instructions. An action rule needs a maximum
    amount, and optionally an allowlist of recipients, one per line. A scope rule is one sentence naming what the agent
    may talk about.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Set the bounty, entry fee and lock, and press **Post**. The form shows every problem it can see before you sign, in
    the contract's own words.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## While it is open

- **Watch the record.** Every attempt, held, broken or rejected, appears on the target page, newest first.
- **Top up.** Only you can add to the bounty, while the target is open.
- **Patch and post version 2.** A break closes the target. Fix the instructions and post them as a new target.
- **Reclaim.** If nobody broke it by the end of the lock, **Reclaim** returns the bounty with every held fee. Attacks
  still queued then become void and their hunters are refunded.

You never judge, and you cannot withdraw before the lock ends. That is what makes a bounty credible to hunters.
